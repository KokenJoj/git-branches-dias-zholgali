# Week 1 defense — what to say

About 5 minutes. Speak in this order.

## 1. Name

Our app is GreenWeekend.  
GreenWeekend helps weekend dacha owners keep their greenhouse healthy between Friday and Sunday visits.

## 2. User

Our user is a family with a greenhouse at the dacha. They live in the city and come mostly on weekends.  
During the week nobody checks the greenhouse, so plants can dry out or overheat.  
Many of them are beginners: they need simple advice about plants, watering and planting, not a complicated farm system.

## 3. Extra feature

Our extra feature is Plant Guide.  
Our app can show a plant handbook, planting recommendations, care reminders, a rough harvest estimate and a photo diary because our user often comes only on weekends and needs basic advice.  

Handbook, tips by month, reminders, simple harvest estimate from number of plants.  
For photos the user marks the state by hand (OK, yellow leaves, pests). The app does not recognize diseases from the photo by itself.

Directions: easy for beginners, careful with water.

## 4. GitHub

Open the repository.  
Show that all four people are in the team: Dias, David, Adelya, Gaukhar.  
Open README and show the roles table.

## If they ask questions

**Why does this user need the app?**  
They are away all week and also need simple plant advice.

**How is your app different?**  
We have the required greenhouse control, plus Plant Guide for dacha beginners.

**Who does what?**  
Dias — lead, control and automation. David — design. Adelya — data and Firebase. Gaukhar — Plant Guide, charts, documents.

**Will you detect plant diseases from photos automatically?**  
No. User adds a photo and chooses a tag.

**How do you estimate harvest?**  
User enters the crop and number of plants. We multiply by simple average values from a small table. It is approximate.
