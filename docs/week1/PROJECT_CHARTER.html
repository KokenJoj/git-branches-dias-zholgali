1. App name and one-sentence description

Name: GreenWeekend

Description: GreenWeekend helps weekend dacha owners keep their greenhouse healthy between Friday and Sunday visits.

2. Target user

Our primary user is a family that keeps a small vegetable greenhouse at a dacha (for example near a city in Kazakhstan) and lives in the city during the work week. They usually visit only on weekends. Midweek they cannot check sensors; beginners also struggle with what to plant, when to water, and how much harvest to expect.

They need a simple phone app for live status, remote control, plant advice, reminders, and a rough harvest estimate — not complex farm software. Secondary users are family members on shared greenhouse accounts.

3. Own extra feature

Plant Guide (Справочник и уход)

Our app can give planting recommendations, a plant handbook, care reminders, an approximate harvest estimate, and a photo diary with health tags because our user is often a beginner who visits only on weekends and needs clear advice, not only sensor numbers.

  1. Handbook — tomato, cucumber, pepper, greens: ideal ranges and short care tips.
  2. Recommended plantings — by season/month for a typical dacha greenhouse.
  3. Reminders — watering, planting, harvest windows.
  4. Harvest estimate — crop + plant count → approximate yield range (simple tables, not AI).
  5. Photo diary — photo + tags (OK / yellow leaves / pests). Full AI disease detection is out of scope for 6 weeks.

This is not a copy of M1–M8: it is the educational / planning layer on top of required modules.

4. Direction (track)

  1. Beginner-friendly UX / education — handbook, recommendations, plain-language reminders.
  2. Water-conscious automation — irrigate when soil is low; reminders discourage overwatering.
